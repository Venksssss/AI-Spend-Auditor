# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Exact Target User

Not "startups" — specifically:
**Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5-20 person SaaS startup,
Series A or bootstrapped, burning $2k-10k/month on AI tools,
who signs the invoices but has never audited the stack.**

They use Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and ChatGPT Team simultaneously
without knowing if there is overlap. They renew subscriptions
automatically. They would switch tools for 30% savings but
nobody has shown them the numbers.

## What They Google Before Wanting This Tool

- "cursor vs github copilot which is better 2025"
- "chatgpt team plan worth it"
- "reduce saas spend startup"
- "ai tools for developers cost comparison"
- "cursor business plan vs pro difference"

## First 100 Users in 30 Days — $0 Budget

### Week 1: Seeding
- Post on r/SaaS: "I built a free tool that audits your AI spend 
  — found $400/mo in savings for my own team"
- Post on Hacker News Show HN: "Show HN: Free AI spend auditor 
  for dev teams"
- Share in 3 Indie Hackers Slack channels with real savings screenshot

### Week 2: Founder Outreach
- Cold DM 50 founders on X who have tweeted about AI tool costs
- Message: "saw your tweet about Cursor pricing — built a free 
  audit tool, took me 2 min to find $300/mo savings, want the link?"
- Post in Lenny's Slack #tools channel with a real audit screenshot

### Week 3: Content
- Write a Twitter thread: "We audited our AI spend and found 
  $500/mo wasted. Here is what we cut and why."
- Submit to newsletters: TLDR, Bytes.dev, JavaScript Weekly
- Post on LinkedIn targeting engineering managers

### Week 4: Referral Loop
- Every shared audit URL is a viral loop — the share page has 
  a CTA to run their own audit
- Add "Audited by AI Spend Auditor" badge to share page
- Ask first 50 users for a tweet in exchange for a PDF export

"Free spend audit for all Cursor users" — would drive 
thousands of signups overnight that no competitor could replicate.
