# Unit Economics

## What Is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Assumptions based on Credex's business model:
- Average AI credit purchase: $2,000/month
- Credex margin on discounted credits: ~20%
- Revenue per customer per month: $400
- Average customer lifetime: 12 months
- **LTV per converted customer: $4,800**

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel

| Channel | Est. CAC | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| HackerNews/Reddit organic | $0 | Time cost only |
| Twitter/X cold DM | $5-10 | ~2hrs per 50 DMs, 2 conversions |
| Newsletter sponsorship | $200-400 | $500 CPM, 0.1% conversion |
| Paid social (future) | $150-300 | B2B SaaS benchmark |

**Blended CAC estimate: $50-100** in early stage with organic focus

## Conversion Funnel
Landing page visit        1,000
Audit completed            400  (40% conversion)
Email captured             160  (40% of completions)
High savings flagged        48  (30% show >$500 savings)
Consultation booked         10  (20% of high savings)
Credit purchase closed       3  (30% close rate)

**Audit → Purchase conversion: 0.3%**
**Revenue per 1,000 visitors: $14,400 LTV**

## What Makes This Profitable

At $50 CAC and $4,800 LTV:
- **LTV:CAC ratio = 96:1**
- Payback period = less than 1 week
- Even at 10x higher CAC ($500), still 9.6:1 LTV:CAC

The tool is free to run (Supabase free tier, Resend free tier,
Vercel free tier) so marginal cost per audit is essentially $0.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

### What Must Be True:

**Month 1-3: Seeding**
- 5,000 audits completed
- 150 consultations booked
- 45 customers converted
- ARR: $45 x $4,800 = $216,000 ARR run rate

**Month 4-9: Growth**
- SEO kicks in for "AI spend audit" keywords
- Referral loop drives 30% of new audits
- 500 audits/month → 15 new customers/month
- ARR grows to $650,000

**Month 10-18: Scale**
- Co-marketing deal with one AI vendor
- 2,000 audits/month
- 60 new customers/month
- **$1M ARR achieved at month 14**

### Key Assumptions That Must Hold:
1. 30% of audits show >$500/mo savings (high savings flag)
2. 20% of high-savings users book a consultation
3. 30% of consultations convert to purchases
4. Average purchase stays at $2,000/month

### What Would Trigger a Pivot:
- If consultation booking rate drops below 5% →
  redesign the results page CTA
- If audit completion rate drops below 25% →
  simplify the form
- If LTV drops below $1,000 →
  reconsider the free tool strategy
