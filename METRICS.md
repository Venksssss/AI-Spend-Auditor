# Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Qualified leads generated per week**
(defined as: audit completed + email captured + savings > $500/mo)

### Why this metric:
This is a B2B lead-gen tool. DAU or total visits are vanity 
metrics — a person who completes an audit, sees significant 
savings, and gives their email is a potential Credex customer.
That's the only metric that maps directly to revenue.

## 3 Input Metrics That Drive the North Star

### 1. Audit Completion Rate
- Definition: % of landing page visitors who complete an audit
- Target: >35%
- Why it matters: If people land but don't complete, the form 
  is too long or confusing. Every % point here multiplies 
  everything downstream.

### 2. High Savings Rate
- Definition: % of completed audits that show >$500/mo savings
- Target: >25%
- Why it matters: Only high-savings audits trigger the Credex 
  CTA and consultation booking. If this is low, the audit 
  engine rules need improvement.

### 3. Email Capture Rate
- Definition: % of audit completions that result in email capture
- Target: >40%
- Why it matters: Email is the lead. No email = no follow-up = 
  no Credex sale. This measures how well the results page 
  sells the value of saving the report.

## What We Would Instrument First

1. **Audit completion funnel** — track drop-off at each form step
   to see which tool input causes abandonment
2. **Results page scroll depth** — do users see the email capture 
   form or bounce before reaching it?
3. **Share link clicks** — are shared audits driving new visitors?
4. **Consultation booking rate** — % of high-savings emails that 
   book a Credex call

## What Number Triggers a Pivot Decision

If after 500 audits:
- Audit completion rate < 20% → simplify the form
- Email capture rate < 20% → move email gate earlier or add more value
- High savings rate < 10% → audit engine rules are too conservative
- Consultation booking rate < 5% → redesign the Credex CTA

**The pivot trigger is 500 audits with less than 5 qualified 
leads generated. At that point the core value proposition 
needs rethinking, not just the UI.**
