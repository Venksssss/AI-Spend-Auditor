# User Interviews

Three interviews conducted during Week 1 with potential users
of the AI Spend Auditor tool. Conducted via WhatsApp and 
in-person conversations.

---

## Interview 1

**Name:** A.R.
**Role:** College student
**Company Stage:** Individual, personal use
**Date:** 2025-05-07
**Duration:** ~10 minutes

**Summary:**
A college student who uses ChatGPT daily for assignments and 
studying. Has never paid for any AI tool and uses only the 
free tier. Treats ChatGPT as a default tool for everything 
regardless of the task.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "Like, always ChatGPT — I just open it for everything"
- "I don't even think about whether there's something better"
- "I didn't know there were other options honestly"

**Most Surprising Thing They Said:**
Regardless of the type of work — writing, coding, math — they 
always open ChatGPT without considering if another tool might 
be better suited. Pure habit, zero evaluation.

**What It Changed About My Design:**
Made me realize the tool needs to explain WHY one tool is better 
for a specific use case, not just show price differences. Added 
the "primary use case" selector to the form to give more 
personalized recommendations.

## Interview 2

**Name:** K.S.
**Role:** Web developer at an early-stage startup
**Company Stage:** Startup, 5-10 person team
**Date:** 2025-05-07
**Duration:** ~10 minutes

**Summary:**
A working developer whose company pays for Claude. Uses it 
heavily for coding tasks. Has strong opinions about Claude 
being the best tool for development work but has never 
personally evaluated the cost since the company handles billing.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "Claude does an excellent job in my field"
- "Best tool ever for coding honestly"
- "I don't worry about cost, the company pays for it"

**Most Surprising Thing They Said:**
He had no idea what his company was paying per seat or whether 
they were on the right plan. The person using the tool daily 
was completely disconnected from the billing — which is exactly 
the problem this tool solves.

**What It Changed About My Design:**
Confirmed that the target user is the person who signs invoices, 
not the person using the tool. Reinforced the decision to ask 
for "team size" and "seats" separately — the buyer and user 
are often different people.

---

## Interview 3

**Name:** P.M.
**Role:** College student, hobby developer
**Company Stage:** Individual, personal projects
**Date:** 2025-05-07
**Duration:** ~10 minutes

**Summary:**
A college student who pays out of pocket for Trae AI because 
it is cheaper than the alternatives. Made a deliberate cost 
decision but acknowledges the quality tradeoff. Has thought 
more about AI tool pricing than most people twice their age.

**Direct Quotes:**
- "It's not the best but it works for what I need"
- "Very cheap and better working at that price point"
- "I compared a few before picking this one"

**Most Surprising Thing They Said:**
Unlike the other two, this person had actually done their own 
manual comparison before choosing a tool. But they did it once 
and never revisited it — they didn't know prices change or that 
better deals emerge. Cheap options are not always worse, but 
they need ongoing re-evaluation.

**What It Changed About My Design:**
Added the "notify me when new optimizations apply to your stack" 
signup for users who are already spending optimally. People like 
this want to stay informed, not just get a one-time audit.
